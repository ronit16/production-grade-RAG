Acme Corp Refund Policy

Acme Corp offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on all products purchased through our website or authorized resellers. To be eligible for a refund, the item must be unused and in its original packaging.

To initiate a return, contact our support team at support@acmecorp.example.com with your order number. Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the status of your refund. Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.

Shipping costs are non-refundable. If you receive a refund, the cost of return shipping will be deducted from your refund. Items purchased during promotional sales may only be exchanged and are not eligible for cash refunds.

For defective or damaged products, please contact us immediately. We will arrange a free replacement or full refund at no additional cost.
